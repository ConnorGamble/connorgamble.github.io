title: Game Reviews
---
Every review follows the **DUCK** format: Describe, Unpack, Critique, Keepers.

## Describe
A quick intro into what the game is and my expectations going into it.

## Unpack
The lengthy section which gets into the nitty gritty of the game. Mechanics, balancing, visuals etc.

## Critique
A quick pros and cons list.

## Keepers
Things which will stick with me about the game.
